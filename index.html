FAQ

How many photos can I add?

As many as you like per area (they're compressed to keep the report light). Typical move-in reports run 20–60 photos across all areas.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes — open the page, tap to add photos straight from your camera roll, then print/share the PDF. Works offline once loaded.

Is a signed report legally binding?

A signed condition report is strong evidence in deposit disputes in most jurisdictions (some states require itemized documentation). This tool produces the document — check your local deposit law for specifics.
